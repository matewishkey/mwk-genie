Your one password

The only thing here that nobody can reset for you.

What it is

Everything you save with mwk — website logins for your projects, keys that let your programs talk to other services — is locked in one file on this computer. One password opens it. Nothing is stored anywhere else, and nobody else has a copy.

That means there is no "forgot password" link. Not from us, not from anyone. It is not that we will not reset it — it is that we cannot. That is the same reason nobody else can open your keys, so it is a feature and a risk at the same time.

Where to keep it

In a password manager. Not in a note, not in a photo of a piece of paper, and not in a file called passwords on your desktop.

  • Already on your computerApple Passwords on a Mac, or the one built into your web browser. Free, already there, and good enough.
  • BitwardenFree, works on every device. A common first one.
  • 1PasswordPaid. Nicer to use, and shares well with a partner or a team.

Any of them beats no password manager. Do not spend a week choosing — pick the one already on your computer today, and change your mind later if you want to.

Five rules that are actually worth following

  1. Make the entry before you make the password. Open the password manager, create a new item called something like Mate Wish Key, and have it waiting. Saving afterwards is how a password ends up in a text file "just for a minute".
  2. Let something else generate it. A password you invented is shorter and more guessable than you think. Your password manager has a generate button; so does mwk if you press Enter when it asks.
  3. Never reuse it. This one password opens everything you save here. If it is also your email password, then anyone who gets your email gets all of it.
  4. Never type it into a chat, an email, or into me. Anything you type to an AI is part of the conversation. If it needs typing, it gets typed in your own terminal window, where nothing is watching.
  5. Stop sharing your screen first. On a call, recording, streaming — the password appears on screen once. That is the one mistake here that cannot be taken back.

If it goes wrong

Somebody saw it, or you think they might have. Change it. One command, takes a second, and the old one stops opening anything.

mwk rekey

You lost it. There is no way back into what is already saved. Delete the store and start again — you will have to re-enter the keys, but nothing else is lost. Ask me and I will walk you through it.
